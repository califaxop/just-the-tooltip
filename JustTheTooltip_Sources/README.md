# Just the Tooltip!: Sources

Data for Just the Tooltip!'s "where it comes from" lines on gear. It loads only when you hover gear
with that option on.

- Quest rewards, creature drops, vendors and chests: [QuestieDB](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB)'s
  WoW: Forever database, by the Questie team and contributors, copied under the GNU General Public
  License v3 as the Questie team allows (see QuestieDB's LICENSE.md). This addon isn't made by or
  affiliated with Questie.
- Professions: the game client's own tables (via wago.tools).
- Drop chances: [CMaNGOS Classic-DB](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), by the CMaNGOS project,
  under the GNU General Public License v3: the Classic 1.12 loot tables (WoW: Forever's may differ).
  This addon isn't made by or affiliated with CMaNGOS.

`Data.lua` is generated by `tools/sources/build.py` in the Just the Tooltip! repository; don't edit it
by hand. This folder (the data and this addon) is licensed under the GPL v3 (see LICENSE).
